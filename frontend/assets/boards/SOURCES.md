# Testbed Hardware Photography

The board photographs were supplied by the project owner from the `nsdi26/borad`
research assets on 2026-09-10. Web copies are resized without altering hardware.
`testbed-hero.webp` is a composition of the supplied Meles, AGX and Raspberry Pi
photographs. Images illustrate device models, not live operational status.

No external image service receives private research files. Additional reuse or
redistribution must respect the original photographs' rights.
